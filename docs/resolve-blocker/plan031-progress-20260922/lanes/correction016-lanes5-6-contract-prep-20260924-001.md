## Review correction — output creation and acyclic bindings

Before admission, bind six distinct canonical *absolute path strings* beneath the still-absent attempt path, and verify that none exists or aliases another. The capture creates the attempt parent exclusively under the reviewed driver procedure. Only after that parent exists does the runner create each lane child exclusively and bind its real identity to its assigned lane and receipt. No lane worker creates the parent or reuses a child from an earlier attempt.

The earlier proposed manifest/admission/session-proof cross-hashing was cyclic and is also rejected. The corrected dependency order is **immutable source manifest → session proof → admission → six lane receipts → final index**. The proof binds the exact returned handle and prospective admission *path*, without an admission digest. Admission binds the manifest digest, the already written strict proof record/hash, and the six absent absolute path strings. Each receipt can bind its assigned path and predecessor records; the terminal index can bind all six receipts. This order follows the current Plan049 session-proof handoff and the launch contract: `launch-049-exec.py` obtains `proof_record` and validates `session_proof` before comparing the admission bindings, while `s1_validation_contract.py` requires `session_proof` in those bindings. Neither manifest nor proof may hash the later admission, and the admission cannot hash a future receipt. This is a planning correction only.
