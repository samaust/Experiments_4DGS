# Independent plan review — wrapper correction 009

**Verdict: NEEDS_REVISION.** Reviewed `correction-009.md` at exact SHA-256 `969071c6580f9337e61151952892fee3b3cd7938682e8e381be335a6718b97de`. This is a static review of the plan-only proposal against Plans 047–049, the wrapper C1–C4 objective, and relevant existing creation and Consumer paths. No source, test, admission, runtime, service or probe was changed or executed for this review.

The user's chosen interpretation is coherent in principle: an opaque outer `exec_command` session can be one *logical* H job, and the registry can enforce a numeric `B+max(1,H)≤8` bound over its own admitted jobs. The proposal correctly says this is not a kernel TID, host-wide load, or hidden-wrapper bound, retains uncertainty charges, and preserves the behavioral workload and deadlines. It cannot yet establish that precise invariant across the existing creation topology. The following revisions are required before adoption or source implementation.

1. **Define the outer-session containment and no-double-counting rule.** Section 1 counts the exact outer tool session as H, while section 2 requires registration of process creation and section 3 preserves capture/runner identity. It never states whether the driver/capture process *inside* that opaque session is also B. State explicitly which process identity is covered by its single H charge and is not charged again as B; `execve` replacement of the driver by capture retains that identity and charge. The capture-created runner process is a separate child and must receive its own declared B/H role and reservation before `Popen`. Other independently created children and threads under the driver/capture are likewise separately charged by role. Without this, the same topology can produce two valid-looking totals.

2. **Give a complete B/H role table and reservation transition rule.** “Primary” versus “helper” does not classify Owner's two helpers, supervisor worker, direct scripts, L18 sentinel, helper/race workers, detached grandchildren, capture-created child, native threads, and any test fixture process at creation and throughout cleanup. Define each kind as B or H, and whether a descendant's role follows its parent or its own purpose. Specify the `H=0` reserve at each transition, including an H job's retirement and a B job's admission, and serialize reservation, creation, failed creation, identity registration, wait/join, and release under one gate. The check must use *reserved plus live and unresolved* slots, not just live handles, so two simultaneous creators cannot both pass the same capacity check. Record role and slot ownership immutably for a job even if its parent exits or it reparents.

3. **Specify enforceable mediation of indirect creation.** Existing `create_owned_process` performs a predispatch census and then calls the creator; `register_owned` is separate and occurs after creation. `InvocationRegistry` is process-local. The helper fixture creates a descendant from inside a helper, and native libraries may create threads without Python `threading.Thread.start`. A Main-owned dictionary cannot by itself gate a child process's `Popen`/`posix_spawn`, native thread creation, or detached work. The proposal needs a concrete precreation reservation and parent-to-Main acknowledgment protocol or a fail-closed prohibition for each such path, including native threads, without changing the required scenario behavior or frozen modules. State how a reserved slot and returned handle survive failure before identity read, how registration races with parent exit, and how an uncooperative native creator is detected or proved absent. Environment thread pins alone are not a complete creation gate. If any required path cannot be mediated, the proposal's own conditional fallback applies; it cannot claim the numeric bound for that path.

4. **Make detached Consumer retirement evidence distinct from progress acknowledgment.** Plan049 requires retained full boot/PID/start-tick/PGID identity and attached-to-reparented lineage through actual Consumer acknowledgment, reconciliation, fallback and freeze. A Consumer progress ack establishes data acceptance, not necessarily process death. Define the exact retained descendant handle or owner protocol and terminal proof after reparenting, including the case where the original parent exits and no ordinary `waitpid` is possible. A changed PID start tick, missing descendant, parent exit, or progress ack alone must not release the job; unresolved identity continues to charge and block creation. The existing fixture's descendant launch and L35 scenario are concrete paths that need this distinction.

5. **Keep the C1–C4 gates separate.** The final section correctly says this proposal meets none of them. For clarity, retain their exact dispositions: C1 durable original tool return/handle **not met**; C2 exact event/output/hash and clock evidence **not met**; C3 independent sufficiency of the actual corrected wrapper, evidence, ownership and bound **not met**; C4 fresh source/authority, storage, ownership/capacity, vacancy and high-water preflight **unverified**. An independent *plan* review, including a future PASS, is not C3 runtime/source acceptance. The user-approved logical-job accounting changes C3/C4's resource interpretation only after an exact adopted amendment; it does not supply C1/C2 evidence or clear any launch. Existing same-handle readiness, ADMIT/send/terminal and exact outer return requirements remain.

The proposal's scope preservation is otherwise appropriate: no operational ceiling; original W/C and scenario/protocol deadlines; unchanged 249 methods, 1,028 typed callbacks, 42 scenarios and six direct scripts; the 150GiB and 64MiB limits; CPU-only serial runtime; no new diagnostic index. Its `registered-job/v1` label is necessary and should be used in every future receipt, validator conclusion and capacity claim so the eight-slot statement is never presented as kernel or machine-wide enforcement.
