The current VTE scope is not writable by the sandbox shell; its `cgroup.subtree_control` is empty and it has no leaf directories. Thus direct child-directory writes and post-spawn migration are **not** the candidate. [systemd's control-group interface](https://systemd.io/CONTROL_GROUP_INTERFACE/) says services are spawned by the manager, scopes register processes spawned by others, and raw subtree management needs delegation. The [kernel cgroup v2 documentation](https://docs.kernel.org/admin-guide/cgroup-v2.html) defines the `pids` controller's task/TID accounting and fork/clone limit; migration can leave an over-limit group and `populated=0` proves no live member, not the absence of zombies. These references justify a **transient service candidate**, not proof that Codex's opaque tool session shares its stdin/output/terminal or that all wrappers are contained.
