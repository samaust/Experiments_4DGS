### OS-byte rule still to prove per create

Let `a_i` be the exact encoded argv strings including each terminating NUL and `e_j` the exact encoded `key=value\0` strings of the immutable effective snapshot. The policy requires a canonical length-delimited executable-plus-argv descriptor `D≤16,384`, full environment encoded key/value total `E≤1,048,576` with `envc≤4,096`, each `|a_i|` and `|e_j|` within the **fresh** selected host's per-string rule, and `Σ|a_i|+Σ|e_j|+P_host(argc,envc,auxiliary/platform overhead)≤ARG_MAX_host`. Exact executable and `argv[0]` are distinct. `P_host` must be justified for the actual selected API/host, including pointer/count overhead; it is not established by the loose Audit005 estimate. Audit005 observed `ARG_MAX=2,097,152`, page size 4,096, pointer width 8, and Linux `MAX_ARG_STRLEN=131,072` including NUL at the reviewed host, with header SHA in the authority manifest. Those observations are neither future host limits nor proof that any actual selected environment satisfies even one string rule. The absent canonical framing, `argc` maxima, temp suffix bounds, full environment snapshots and platform overhead make exact per-edge static/slot/vector byte maxima `U`, despite the independent policy ceilings.
