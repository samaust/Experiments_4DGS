## Recommended diagnostic and measurable acceptance

Map all of this work to **SC-01 (timing prerequisite), SC-03 (unblocking Basketball measurements), and SC-05 (reproducible bounded evidence)**.

1. Freeze 12 immutable inputs: baseline and metric records for cold conditional-00/07/13 and their cold conditional-03/10/16 controls. Bind their attempt, receipt, journal, execution-source and mathematical-problem hashes; preserve the synthetic fixture, observations, offsets, window, spline, objectives and constraints. Do not rerun baseline or use these fitted states as optimizer seeds.
2. For each input select callback ordinal indices `0`, `floor((n-1)/3)`, `floor(2*(n-1)/3)`, and `n-1`, in stored order: at most **48 saved-state evaluations**. Reconstruct sample trajectories, reprojection objective, regularization contribution, depths, objective gradient and actual-multiplier original KKT; retain transform conversion and sample camera/frame links. Original objective/depth/KKT must agree with applicable saved values at inherited verifier tolerances. Finite callback states and saved multipliers supply evidence; inferred barrier multipliers must not substitute for them.
3. Construct rays only from the six **metric** inputs, at the exact returned physical state, with two directions: returned minus first callback, and returned minus the selected two-thirds callback. Test each direction and its negative, giving at most **24 rays**. Normalize by maximum absolute sampled XYZ displacement, preserving every coefficient. Freeze exact bytes and equations before any ray evaluation. An exactly zero displacement is a retained degenerate slot, not permission to choose another direction. Do not change offsets, delete weak/null coefficients, impose coefficient bounds or add regularization.
4. Use the existing finite amplitude ladder `10**e` for even `e=0..44`: **23 slots per ray, at most 552 new-state evaluations**. Before each objective entry, check finite arithmetic and original depths. Retain infeasible/nonfinite slots without optimizer restoration or replacements. Derive each ray's analytical fixed-offset projective limit and depth slopes; for weight-one controls include the acceleration term and distinguish divergent from invariant acceleration. Preserve distinctions between exact compact-support zeros and small nonzero slopes. A large finite endpoint need not equal the limit when sample depth slopes are small; record that uncertainty rather than extending the ladder.
5. Independently re-evaluate each applicable snapshot/probe once and verify classifications, exact identities and counts. Compare each ray's finite costs and applicable limiting cost against its own same-problem saved returned cost using the inherited `1e-6 + 1e-4*max(abs(F1),abs(F2))` tolerance. Publish whether a ray is infeasible, exactly unobservable, has a noncompetitive limit, has a competitive feasible finite limit, diverges, or remains unresolved, with the actual finite-probe trend. Do not require that one favored explanation wins to complete the diagnostic.

Acceptance is a complete, hash-bound per-case diagnosis and independent verification with explicit unknowns, within the finite limits. A feasible competitive ray is evidence of an available direction with bounded limiting loss; even decreasing loss along that ray is **not proof that no finite minimizer exists**, that the solver must escape, or that outer timing is unidentifiable. Conversely, finite noncompetitive rays do not exclude other directions. The old growth flag and scientific qualification thresholds remain unchanged.

The deliverable must state which explanation is supported for each failed case and recommend one consequent next implementation or mathematical investigation, with its required budget. The present plan must not launch that follow-on work, bundle in interior initialization, or secretly turn evaluator probes into optimization. Preserve `ready_for_full_screens=false`, accepted timing, production candidate and final-validation protocol null.
