# Publish the consolidated depth-model survey

## Parent

[Issue #36 — Survey depth estimation models and compare published evidence](https://github.com/samaust/Experiments_4DGS/issues/36)

## What to build

Give the user one compact report answering what depth models are available,
how they compare under documented paper protocols, what their commercial terms
allow, and which deserve future experiments in this project. Integrate the six
complete research sections into one consistent inventory and evidence package,
reconcile overlap and contradictions, and explain coverage and unresolved gaps.

The final assessment uses the user-confirmed report/source review boundary.
It recommends future tests and does not execute them. Use the existing section
verification records; recheck changed or conflicting evidence during integration.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All six prerequisite sections and their source-check records are complete and their discovered cross-category leads have explicit dispositions.
- [ ] The report includes a concise findings overview, model availability inventory, protocol-grouped performance tables, four-column commercial-use findings, project tradeoffs, future-test shortlists, cutoff, coverage and references.
- [ ] The combined search record accounts for every supplied entry point, two rounds of citation expansion in the research categories, inclusion/exclusion reasons and screened-source counts. A targeted final pass addresses material remaining gaps, with unresolved evidence reported explicitly.
- [ ] Model aliases, wrappers, family/variant distinctions, source identifiers and duplicate observations are reconciled. D0/D1 remain implementation controls in one family. Distinct variants and conflicting source values are preserved rather than silently collapsed.
- [ ] Consolidated search, model, benchmark and license records are readable and consistently linked, with documented field meanings, units, metric directions and missing-value conventions. Report numbers and conclusions trace to these records and their exact primary sources.
- [ ] Every ranking is limited to compatible settings. Separate metric, relative, temporal, multi-view and efficiency evidence is retained; incompatible scores are not averaged into a universal winner.
- [ ] All shortlisted variants have verified release identities and separate code, weight, inference/output and explicit commercial-offer findings, including stated uncertainty and documented material dependency qualifications.
- [ ] Shortlists cover metric scale, detail, video consistency and commercial use, allowing ties or empty categories when warranted. Every proposed future test has an evidence-based reason and unresolved question relevant to calibrated Basketball footage.
- [ ] Published measurements, author claims, report inferences and existing local human/experimental evidence remain distinct. Missing temporal or project-specific evidence is not presented as a measured strength or weakness.
- [ ] Final review confirms coverage, source traceability, compatible comparisons, document/link/data integrity and justification of recommendations. Reuse completed source checks and recheck integration changes or contradictions; document any remaining limitations.
- [ ] The completed report and supporting records are committed as a research milestone. The work remains within the parent's literature-only scope and does not change historical experiment evidence or close the parent issue.
