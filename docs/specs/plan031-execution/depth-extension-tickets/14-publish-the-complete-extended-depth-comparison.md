- [ ] Check the final viewer's matching frames, synchronized motion controls, crops and diagnostics; publish exact package/media hashes and the review handoff.
- [ ] Deliver all D0–D11 dispositions and cost/scale evidence for the existing final report, preserving the distinction between component appearance, scale consistency and unseen final renders.
