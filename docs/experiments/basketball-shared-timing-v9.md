# Basketball shared timing v9: accounting correction and a blocked focused benchmark

[Plan 015](../../plans/plan_015.md) ends with a **computational blocker**. The historical
accounting audit, canonical ledger regressions and saved-state diagnosis completed. The first
fresh baseline then failed to serialize its detailed attempt records. Its completion events
survived, but its fitted states and numerical-entry ledgers did not. No adaptation arm or final
benchmark ran, and this investigation is **not ready for full screens**.

This is an implementation/persistence failure, not evidence that a numerical adaptation failed.
The serialization bug was repaired and tested without rerunning scientific attempts. No policy
was tuned from these baseline outcomes. Accepted timing, production candidate and final-validation
protocol remain null.

## Accounting correction

The [separate v8 correction](basketball-shared-timing-v9/v8-accounting-correction.md) identifies
two caches with different state identities. Objective calls round-tripped original scaled
coordinates through physical units; Hessian calls used the direct transform. Across all 22
affected attempts, saved callbacks contain 474 distinct-q/same-physical-byte witnesses.
An observation-only derivative sequence reproduces two inner evaluations versus one exported
state. No optimization was needed to explain the mechanism.

V8 did not retain enough information to reconstruct exact complete entry counts. Its original
returned-state objective/depth/KKT and nine historical basin-coverage evidence remain hash-bound;
its conditioning accounting and resulting efficacy interpretation require the qualification in
this supplement. V8 is not rewritten as a pass.

V9 also labels the separate Plan 013 contract repair: symmetric coefficient transform and
destination-cold origin. The objective, observations, full coefficient/null spaces, scale limits,
physical bounds, support thresholds, stationarity gate and scalar refinement policy are preserved.
The canonical ledger charges initialization, derivative-first and constraint-only work before
numerical entry, and owns returned states without another coordinate round trip.

## Diagnosis and frozen adaptations

The deterministic [benchmark manifest](basketball-shared-timing-v9/prepare-resumed/benchmark-manifest.json)
covers all three groups, both weights, all requested failure strata and six successful controls.
Physical problems are deduplicated while all required paths remain. Saved fits are diagnostic
and cost references only; every policy requires fresh cold sources and isolated caches.

The three selected infeasible cold states have minimum normalized depths approximately
−0.262, −0.284 and −0.290. The failed selected data-only states retain rank 48 of 54 in the
reported target diagnostics. Support activation, weak directions and trust-region contraction
coexist; this is not proof of a single cause or permission to remove coefficients.

The three saved `xtol` exits have original-coordinate KKT norms approximately
`2.2932e-6`, `2.3568e-6` and `1.1435e-6`. Their trust radii are below `1e-6`.
The installed source and [SciPy reference](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/optimize.minimize-trustconstr.html)
distinguish trust-radius termination from Lagrangian stationarity. The qualification threshold
remains `1e-6`, including the historically lost near-threshold regularized joint case.

Before the baseline, the [candidate freeze](basketball-shared-timing-v9/diagnose/candidate-freeze.json)
specified one full-coordinate coefficient metric arm, one analytical positive-z feasible cold
construction, and one stopping arm with `xtol=0`. Their screens were fixed independently.
Only individually passing arms could enter one fresh final policy. None of these arms executed,
so feasibility unit tests and derivative tests are not credited as scientific arm passes.
The nine historical multiplier reconstructions and unchanged 172-ray/3,956-probe escape audit
were independently reverified without additional rays or scientific solves.

## Persistence failure and correction

The first baseline used the historical strict JSON writer. New ledger problem metadata retained
NumPy observation arrays, producing `TypeError: Object of type ndarray is not JSON serializable`.
The group writer opened gzip output before JSON encoding, leaving 18 empty gzip streams when
encoding failed. The durable per-attempt events still establish 75 completed invocations and six
missing-seed paths. They cannot recover parameters, signed KKT contributions, or numerical-entry
observations. Reported state counts and reported qualifications therefore are not independently
certified scientific results.

Scientific work stopped. No baseline was retried and no missing fitted state was reconstructed
from a rerun. The [serialization correction](basketball-shared-timing-v9/corrections/serialization.json)
archives exact pre-fix v9 sources and binds corrected sources separately. Problem metadata is now
JSON-safe; the writer validates finite JSON before publication and publishes fresh artifacts
atomically. Tests cover full ledger round trips, NumPy arrays/scalars, nonfinite metadata, encoding
failure without an empty artifact, deterministic bytes and overwrite rejection. This repair does
not repair the lost historical baseline evidence.
